    More for you

    Games for any night (no prep)
    • Alphabet Food: name foods A to Z around the table. Apple, banana, carrot… Stuck = skip.
    • Would You Rather: answer in a full sentence with "because." "I would rather fly because…"
    • My Day in 3 Words: no "good" or "fine." Try busy, hilarious, exhausting.
    • New Word Hunt: each kid brings one new word from a book, show or game to Sunday dinner.
    Tips for you as a parent learning English too
    • You don't need to be the expert. Say: "Let's figure it out together." Kids love being the teacher.
    • Pronunciation is split into parts, like CUR-ee-us. Say the CAPITAL part louder.
    • Ask "How do you say this word?" It makes them think about sounds and spelling.
    • Ask "What is this word in our language?" Knowing two languages is a strength.
    • If someone is tired, skip the game. Just ask: "What's one interesting word you heard today?"
